About the Platform

Automated Face Attendance System

This automated face attendance system uses advanced facial recognition technology to streamline the attendance process. The system provides the following features:

Facial Recognition

Using YOLOv8 and SVM technology for accurate face detection and recognition.

Automatic Attendance

Automatically records check-in times and classifies attendance status.

Statistics and Reports

Detailed reports on your attendance history and performance.

User Management

Manage your profile and view your attendance records.

How to Use the Attendance System
1
Position Your Face

Make sure your face is centered in the camera frame for accurate recognition.

2
Verify Information

When the system recognizes your face and displays your information, check it and press Confirm if correct.

3
Manual Entry

If the information is incorrect, press Reject and enter your employee ID manually.

4
Retry Recognition

If you want to cancel the current process and try again, press Cancel.
